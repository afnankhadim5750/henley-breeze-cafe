Our story

A family cafe with the beach at its doorstep

Henley Breeze Cafe is family-run and proudly local. We open the doors at 6AM so early swimmers, dog walkers and sunrise chasers can start the day with proper coffee and a proper breakfast — right across from the sand at Henley Beach.

Everything is made fresh each morning, the coffee is poured with care, and the view comes free with every table.

  • Beachfront tables on Seaview Road
  • All-day breakfast menu until 2PM
  • Family-run and dog-friendly out front
